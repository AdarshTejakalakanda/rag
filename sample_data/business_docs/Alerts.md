Feature: F1793489: Reach | Expand Manual User Member Alerts (Add 3 New Alerts)

Impacted Applications & Markets: QOM / Reach Portal | National CEQ & WellMed

Objective: Enable Reach/QOM outreach managers to manually create, display, edit, and retire member alerts directly within the member profile left panel to streamline outreach management.

Scope & Categories: Add three manual alert types:

Out of Country

Member request - Hold Outreach

Hospitalized

UI/UX Specifications:

Left Panel: Active alerts display with an orange background and an info icon showing a tooltip with start date, end date, and notes.

Right Panel: "Manage Member Alerts" drawer opens via edit icon; contains mandatory start date, optional end date, notes field, save/cancel actions, and a toggle between User and System alerts.

History Drawer: Inactive/retired alerts display ordered by most recent inactivation timestamp with full audit history (user/system and updated date).
